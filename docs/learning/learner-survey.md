# Learner Survey (audience analysis)

**Owner:** Lead Instructional Designer · **Status:** Draft 1, 2026-11-26 · **Feeds:** [LEARNING.md](../../LEARNING.md) §0.1–0.2

**Purpose.** Replace the *(assumed)* marks in LEARNING.md §0 with answers from target learners. Chapter 9 (coordinator's summary): a direct survey of a few target learners beats assumptions.

**Use in this design exercise.** No real students take part (TEAM.md Decision Log). **Marked for the user's playtests:** the user answers once as themselves (adult tester baseline) and once in each persona (playtest plan Lens A and Lens B), and any future teacher-reviewer can give it to a few students. Answers from personas are design judgment, not data.

**Privacy.** Anonymous: no name, no player ID, no school. Paper or the school's usual form tool. Nothing links answers to the game's logs. Age is asked as a range only.

**Length.** About 10 minutes. Reading level grade 6–7.

---

## A. About you

1. How old are you? ☐ 11–12 ☐ 13–14 ☐ 15–16 ☐ 17–18
2. Which grade are you in? (write in)
3. Which languages do you speak at home? (optional, write in)

## B. Games in general

4. How often do you play video games? ☐ Almost every day ☐ A few times a week ☐ A few times a month ☐ Rarely ☐ Never
5. What kinds of games do you like most? (tick up to 3) ☐ Building / sandbox ☐ Survival ☐ Strategy / management ☐ Story / adventure ☐ Puzzle ☐ Shooter / action ☐ Sports / racing ☐ Party / social ☐ Other: ___
6. On what do you usually play? (tick all) ☐ Computer ☐ Console ☐ Phone or tablet
7. Do you have a computer at home that can run Minecraft Java Edition? ☐ Yes ☐ No ☐ Not sure

## C. Minecraft

8. Have you played Minecraft? ☐ Never ☐ A little ☐ A lot
9. Which kind? (tick all) ☐ Survival ☐ Creative ☐ Minigames / servers ☐ Minecraft Education ☐ Not sure
10. Have you played Minecraft with mods or modpacks (for example machines or tech mods)? ☐ Never ☐ Once or twice ☐ Often
11. How sure are you that you could build a sealed room and craft a machine without help? ☐ Very sure ☐ Somewhat ☐ Not sure

## D. Interests

12. How interested are you in each? (Not at all / A little / A lot) Space and Mars · Survival challenges · Building things · Mysteries · Animals and nature · Earth's climate · Engineering and machines · Working in a team
13. In a game, what makes you want to keep playing? (write in)
14. What makes you quit a game? (write in)

## E. Reading and play style

15. When a game shows a lot of text, what do you usually do? ☐ Read all of it ☐ Read some ☐ Skip it
16. Do you like playing ☐ alone ☐ with friends ☐ either?
17. In a team game, which do you enjoy most? (tick 1) ☐ Planning ☐ Building ☐ Exploring ☐ Protecting the team ☐ Checking the numbers ☐ Talking and organizing

## F. Prior knowledge (not graded; checks LEARNING.md §0.2)

18. Does Mars have air? ☐ No air at all ☐ A little, much thinner than Earth's ☐ About the same as Earth ☐ Not sure
19. Does water always boil at 100 °C? ☐ Yes ☐ No ☐ Not sure. Why? (optional)
20. Is the greenhouse effect the same thing as the hole in the ozone layer? ☐ Yes ☐ No ☐ Not sure

---

**How results change the design.** Q4–11 tune onboarding length and the worked example skip (LEARNING.md 2.2). Q12–14 feed the Lead Game Designer's fun strategy. Q15 checks the reading-time assumption (prompts-and-reflections §5). Q16–17 check colony posts and the Bartle-type risks (LEARNING.md 4.9). Q18–20 check the misconception table; they're not the pre-test.
