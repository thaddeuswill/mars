# Media Analysis

**Owner:** Lead Instructional Designer · **Status:** Draft 1, 2026-11-26 · Fills the "Media analysis" gap in DOCUMENTATION.md §1 · **Depends on:** [LEARNING.md](../../LEARNING.md) §0, §2; [cta.md](cta.md); story bible §6.1

Two questions: **why a Minecraft modpack** for these goals, and **which in-game medium carries which content**.

---

## 1. Why a Minecraft modpack?

| Content type (LEARNING.md 0.3) | What the medium must do | Minecraft modpack | Alternatives considered |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cause and effect (A1–A5, B1) | Let learners act and see the system respond, many times | **Strong**: building is the action, the world is the response (sky, ice, water, plants) | Web simulation (PhET-style): clearer variables, much cheaper; weaker motivation for a long unit and no building |
| Systems and loops (B2, B4, B5) | Several interacting variables over time, with side effects | **Strong** with our Planetary Index | System-dynamics tools: precise, abstract, low appeal for 12-year-olds |
| Prediction (B3) | Commit before seeing the outcome | Good: forecasts in the journal | Any medium can do this |
| Engineering design (C1, C2) | Build, test, fail, rebuild | **Strong**: Minecraft is a building tool | Physical labs: authentic, slow, costly |
| Data use (C3) | Real numbers and trends | Good, with our Monitor | Spreadsheets: stronger for analysis, no stakes |
| Ethics and Earth links (D1, D2) | Discussion, argument, writing | **Weak alone**: needs debriefs and reflections outside the game | Class discussion, writing tasks (we use them) |

**Fit with learners (0.1):** high Minecraft familiarity *(assumed)*; a known control schema lowers extraneous load (DESIGN.md, cognitive load).

**Costs of the medium:** Java Edition only, not Minecraft Education (`docs/tech/school-deployment.md`); school hardware and IT approval; tech-mod interfaces add load (mitigated: learning-critical machines are ours and simple); long play time; text is the main explanation channel (Minecraft has no built-in narration).

**Conclusion:** the medium is strong for the cause-and-effect, systems, and engineering goals that make up most of the content, and weak for D1 and D2, which is why they're carried by debriefs, reflections, and the transfer task outside the game. The cost is reach (Java-only schools, clubs, home).

## 2. Which in-game medium carries what

| Medium | Carries | Best for | Not for | Goals |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **The world** (sky, fog, ice, water, flora, fauna, sound) | What happened | Natural consequences; visible progress | Why it happened; numbers | All, especially A1, A2, B5 |
| **Planetary Monitor** | Values, trends, causes, next gate, Air window | Data, cause lines, progress | Long explanations | C3, B1, B3, A1, A5, B4 |
| **Weather mast** | The same values as world instruments | An honest world cue for invisible gases | Precision | A1, A5, C3 |
| **Colony log** (BELL) | Short event lines | Immediate outcome feedback | Teaching | B1, C2 |
| **Failure reports** | Cause + one hint | Process feedback on failure | Full answers | C2, B1 |
| **Forecasts** | Prediction and result | Retrieval, prediction, misconceptions | Long reasoning | B3, A2–A5, C1 |
| **Journal entries** | Real science, fiction notes, "On Earth" | Just-in-time explanation | Anything time-critical | A1–A5, D2 |
| **Recorded messages** | Relationships, the D1 voices, Earth links | Motivation, ethics, D2 | Instructions | D1, D2 |
| **Shift Briefings** | The phase's big idea and what to watch | Advance organizing | Detail | All, by phase |
| **Hana's notes** | Worked example | Procedures and where to look | Repeat use by experts | B1, C3 |
| **Keeper's Reports** | After Action Review + reflection | Reflection, written evidence | Quick feedback | B2, B4, B5, C2, D1, D2 |
| **Keeper's Record** | What the player has shown | Self-assessment | Grading | All |
| **Shift Board** | Who claimed and fixed what | Coordination; individual C2 evidence | Scoring people against each other | C2 |
| **Outside the game:** debriefs, worksheets, pre/post, transfer task | Discussion, Earth data, assessment | D1, D2, transfer, summative measures | — | All |

**Rules carried from research:** pair words with visuals; text next to what it explains; one idea per line; sound only for context and danger, always subtitled (DESIGN.md, Multimedia).
