**Stage** = the ROADMAP stage that first needs it: **2** prototype, **3** vertical slice (Phase 1), **4** production (Phases 2–3), **5+** polish. Sizes per style guide §5.1. Paths: `pack/assets/mars/textures/` (confirmed by the TL, ARCHITECTURE.md §3.2; the folders exist).

### Fidelity plan (Chapter 9: straw-man → concept → final)

Assets cost a lot of effort, and their **physical fidelity** should match what the learning goals and the players' expertise need, not more (TEAM.md §3: moderate physical fidelity; DESIGN.md Fidelity levels). Each row says which steps it goes through:

| Code | Step | What it is | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| **SM** | Straw-man | Boxes, circles, flat colors, text boxes, magenta corner (style guide §5.5). Made by script in hours | Stage 2 tests core logic (does pressure rise; can players read the Monitor) without art effort |
| **C** | Concept | A sketch or generated image for **owner and SME face-validity review** before finals ("does this look like what it claims to be?") | Catch wrong science or wrong tone before paying for finals |
| **F-E** | Final, **essential** | Full care: carries a learning cue, is seen constantly, or is the emotional peak. Passes every numeric check (colorblind, grayscale, contrast on its real background) | These are where fidelity teaches |
| **F-S** | Final, **simple** | Functional and on-style, low effort: simple shapes, shared bases, ramps reused. Can stay close to the straw-man | Detail here would be extraneous load and cost |

**Current state:** nothing is Final. **Phase 1 straw-men exist** (batch 1: T-01, M-07, M-08, M-10, M-13; batch 2: M-05 rebuilt; batch 3: M-09 round panel (five dust stages), M-09a partly cleaned, M-10 real-scale battery; all with a 1 px placeholder marker): textures, block and item models, and blockstates in `pack/assets/mars/`, generated by `tools/art-preview/gen_phase1_batch1.py`; preview renders and contact sheet in [renders/phase1-batch1/](renders/phase1-batch1/). Other files made so far: the palette sheet (placeholder), five concept images in [concepts/](concepts/) (generated by script), and the cloud density map `pack/assets/mars/textures/environment/clouds_density.png` (generated concept).
