One evaluator's pass (Claude). Skills run: game-visual-critique, ux-laws, game-interaction-review, adapted to a Minecraft GUI screen at 1280x720, GUI scale 3 (426 x 240 GUI px). No Java or pack files changed. No client run started.

## Evidence and what is verified
- **Verified (looked at the PNGs):** `tools/screenshots/out/20261127-112714/slice3_powered_one_panel.png`, `slice3_short_two_outgassers_dusty_panel.png`, `slice3_unpowered_5_sols.png`; `20261127-110408/slice1_monitor_start.png`. Code: `MonitorScreen.java`. Wording: `en_us.json`, `ui-specs.md` §9.

All pass 4.5:1. EDGE 6B4A36 vs backing = 2.2:1 and vs sky 2.18:1 (below the 3:1 UI-part target, minor: the border is decoration only).

- Font: the shots show a pixel font at scale 3 (label about 24 px on screen, value 1.5x). Whether this is the final font is Stage 3 (code comment). Not checked.

## State table (Monitor as an interaction)
States: no planet -> (monitor.no_planet text) | open, read-only | closed via Esc. Rows appear as values come into play (ui-specs §1). Guards: none block the player; the only failure is the unexplained 0.00 rate (finding 1). Way out of every state: Esc. Not built: paused, unanswered-forecast-first (CR-F9).

## Playtest plan (short; check against real players, not just this pass)
Questions: (1) Can a player say why pressure is not rising? (2) Do they find the Power row first or last? (3) Does the panel hide what they want to see?
Tasks (target time): T1 place Outgasser and Monitor, read pressure (2 min); T2 say what "No power" means and fix it (3 min); T3 find why one panel gives less (2 min, dust); T4 find Next goal (1 min). Metrics: success, time, blocked looks (staring more than 30 s), SEQ 1-7. 5 players: 2 fans, 2 casual, 1 domain expert; pilot with one first.

## Suggested order for the Technical Lead
1 (reasons on cause lines), 2 (size budget, owner request), 3 (one emphasis row), 6 (Esc hint, empty state), then 4, 5, 7, 8. Re-shoot at 1280x720 after each; compare against the numbers above (target <= 132 GUI px tall, <= 55% of screen).
